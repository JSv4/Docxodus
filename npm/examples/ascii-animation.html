THE CANVAS PARAGRAPH, LIVE (session.raw.getXml)


  


  

    How it works: a blank DOCX is minted in-memory (CreateBlankDocx) and seeded through the
    agentic editing surface (replaceText, insertParagraph, applyFormat,
    insertFootnote). One paragraph is the framebuffer: every animation frame builds that
    paragraph's OOXML — colored monospace runs plus w:br line breaks — and swaps it in with
    session.raw.replaceXml. Because the replacement XML keeps the paragraph's Unid, the anchor
    survives every frame, so session.renderBlock(anchor) re-renders just that block and the DOM
    swap is one replaceWith. Hit Save .docx mid-wave and open it in Word: the sea
    freezes exactly where you caught it.
